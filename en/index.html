Privacy

Your health is yours alone.

Sihtak is built for families who trust it with their most private details, so the rules stay simple and clear.

No ads, no data selling

There are no ads in the app, and we never sell your data to anyone.

Fingerprint or face lock

Your data travels encrypted, and you can lock the app with your fingerprint or face.

Delete your account any time

From Settings or the account deletion page, and your data goes with it.

AI only when you ask

The assistant reads your record only with your consent, and you can switch that off in Settings.
