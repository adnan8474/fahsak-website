Frequently asked questions

Is Sihtak free?

Yes. Sihtak is completely free for everyone, with no ads, and we never sell your data.

How does Sihtak remind me to take my medicines?

Add the medicine, its dose and its times, and you get a notification at every dose. Log it with one tap, follow how well you keep to it week by week, and get an alert when the medicine is running low.

How does Sihtak explain lab results?

Photograph the report with your phone camera. Sihtak reads the values, lays them out with their normal ranges, explains each one in plain language and compares it with your earlier results. The explanation is there to help you understand; diagnosis is for your doctor.

What can the health assistant do?

It answers general health questions in Arabic or English, by text or voice, and takes your listed medicines and results into account if you allow it. It does not diagnose or prescribe, and it points you to a doctor when you need one.

Can Sihtak read data from my watch or phone?

Yes, with your permission. It reads steps, sleep and heart rate from Health Connect on Android and Apple Health on iPhone, which also carry Garmin data. WHOOP and Oura support is coming soon.

Which countries does Sihtak work in?

Sihtak works across the Arab world and beyond, on Android and iPhone. The clinic directory and booking are live in cities where clinics have joined, and grow as more do.

Is my health data safe?

Your data travels encrypted and you can lock the app with your fingerprint or face. There are no ads, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account and its data at any time.

I run a clinic or a lab. How do I join?

The professional account is free for clinics and labs. Create your profile on the Sihtak portal at portal.sihtak.com and manage bookings and patient records from your phone or browser.

Does Sihtak replace my doctor?

No. Sihtak helps you understand, organise and remember; medical decisions stay with your doctor.
