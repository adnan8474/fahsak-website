Convert creatinine between µmol/L and mg/dL

Many labs report creatinine in micromoles per litre (µmol/L), others in mg/dL. Enter the number and pick a direction.

--

This tool is for general awareness and estimation only. It does not diagnose or replace a doctor. Results are estimates and your doctor interprets them for your situation.

How does this tool work?

1 mg/dL of creatinine equals 88.4 µmol/L. Divide µmol/L by 88.4 to get mg/dL, and multiply mg/dL by 88.4 for the reverse.

If your result is in mmol/L, multiply by 1000 first to get µmol/L. For example, 0.088 mmol/L is 88 µmol/L, about 1.0 mg/dL.

Normal creatinine varies between labs and between men and women, and depends on muscle mass, so compare with the range printed on your report. Doctors usually judge kidney function by the eGFR, calculated from creatinine, age and sex, rather than by the number alone.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 mg/dL of creatinine in µmol/L?

88.4 µmol/L.

My result is in mmol/L. How do I convert it?

Multiply by 1000 to get µmol/L, then divide by 88.4 to get mg/dL.

Does creatinine alone tell me how my kidneys are working?

No. Doctors use the eGFR and other tests such as protein in the urine, and interpret them for your situation.
